Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

What is stored

Nikflix does not use user accounts or a backend database. Your profile name, avatar color, playback settings, My List, and watch progress are saved locally in your browser using localStorage.

What we don't collect

We do not run tracking scripts, ad pixels, or third-party analytics. Form submissions on the help page stay in your browser session.

External APIs & embeds

When you search or open a series, your browser may fetch episode info from TVmaze or public-domain media from Internet Archive. Embedded video streams and trailers load directly from their respective providers.

Deleting your data

You can clear My List and viewing history anytime from the Profile page, or clear site data in your browser settings.
