Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

What we store

Nikflix doesn't have accounts or a server database. Your profile name, avatar colour, preferences, My List and recently viewed titles are saved in your own browser using local storage.

What we don't collect

We don't use tracking cookies, advertising pixels or analytics. Messages sent through the contact form on this demo are not uploaded anywhere.

Third-party services

When you search, Nikflix may request extra series information from TVmaze. Trailers open on YouTube, which has its own privacy policy. Movie descriptions come from Wikipedia.

Deleting your data

You can clear My List and your history from the Profile page, or clear this site's data in your browser settings at any time.
