Frequently asked questions

What is Nikflix?

Nikflix is a personal streaming web app built for browsing and watching movies, TV series, and anime in one place. You can search by title, genre, or year, switch streaming servers inside the player, and save favourites to My List.

How do I save a title to My List?

Open any movie or series and tap + My List. Everything you save shows up on your Profile page and in the "My List" row on Home.

Where are my profile and list stored?

Directly in your browser's local storage on your device. Clearing browser data or using an incognito window will reset them. See our Privacy Policy.

How do I browse by genre?

Tap Categories in the bottom bar to explore Anime, Horror, Romance, Tragedy, Action, Comedy, Sci-Fi, and more. You can filter each category by movies or series.

What if a video stream is slow or doesn't load?

Open the right-hand panel inside the video player and switch between available servers under the Server tab (Direct Stream, YouTube, VidSrc, SuperEmbed, or AutoEmbed).
