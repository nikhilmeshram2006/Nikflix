Frequently asked questions

What is Nikflix?

Nikflix is a fan-made, Netflix-style demo website. You can browse movies, series and anime, search by title, genre or year, and save favourites to My List. It shows trailers from YouTube but does not stream full movies or episodes.

How do I save a title to My List?

Open any movie or series and tap + My List. Everything you save appears on your Profile page and in the “My List” row on Home.

Where are my profile and list stored?

On your device only, in your browser's local storage. Clearing your browser data, or using a private window, will reset them. See our Privacy Policy.

How do I browse by genre?

Tap Categories in the bottom bar to explore Anime, Horror, Romance, Tragedy, Action, Comedy, Sci-Fi and more. You can filter each category by movies or series.

Why can I find series that aren't on the Home page?

When you're online, Search also looks up extra series from TVmaze, so you can find almost any TV show — not just the ones featured on Home.
